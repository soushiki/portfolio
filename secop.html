Step 3 of 4

Offices could tick a box saying "this adds money". They ticked it 14% of the time.

That is 2,609 of 18,670 amendments whose text describes adding money. I wrote rules that read the text. Try them yourself.

Sort it yourself

These are real texts. Pick the class each one belongs to.

  1. “ADICIONAL EN TIEMPO Y VALOR No. 1 AL CONTRATO…”

    “Addition in time and value No. 1 to the contract…”

    Adds money: it says “additional” next to valor (value).

  2. “Se adiciona por la suma de $4.680.000…”

    “It is added for the sum of $4,680,000…”

    Adds money: it says something is added and gives a sum in pesos.

  3. “ADICIONAL 1 CPS-302-2023”

    “Addition 1, contract CPS-302-2023”

    Unclear: it names an addition but does not say if it is money or time.

  4. “No fue necesario realizar modificaciones adicionales”

    “It was not necessary to make additional modifications”

    Other: this is the false alarm the rules must skip. “Modificaciones adicionales” is not an addition.

Of every 100 amendments, about 18 add money. Almost none were labelled that way.

Show the evidence

How the rule decides

  • Adds money: the official label "Adicion en el valor", or a text that names an addition next to money words (valor, $, pesos, suma, CDP, presupuesto, recursos).
  • Adds money too: "adición y prórroga", because in Colombian contracting the adición is the money part.
  • Unclear: names an addition but does not say money or time. It counts only toward the 20.0% ceiling.
  • Time only: "adición en el plazo", months or days with no money words.
  • Other: a negation ("sin adición", "no se realizó adición") or "modificaciones adicionales".

The false alarm: "No fue necesario realizar modificaciones adicionales" contains the word "adicionales" but is not an addition. The cases are pinned in tests/test_clean.py.

Step 4 of 4

Most contracts are awarded directly, but most of the money goes through competitions.

A direct award is when a body picks the supplier without a competition. It is legal in cases set by law.

Show the evidence

These queries are in the repo's sql/ folder, trimmed here to the essentials.

Chart 3: how often contracts grow (sql/03_adiciones_por_modalidad.sql)

WITH m AS (
  SELECT id_contrato,
         SUM(clase = 'adicion_valor') AS n_valor,
         SUM(clase IN ('adicion_valor', 'adicion_sin_detalle')) AS n_max
  FROM adiciones GROUP BY id_contrato
)
SELECT c.modalidad_familia AS modalidad, COUNT(*) AS contratos,
  ROUND(100.0 * SUM(COALESCE(m.n_valor, 0) > 0) / COUNT(*), 1) AS pct_con_adicion,
  ROUND(100.0 * SUM(COALESCE(m.n_max, 0) > 0) / COUNT(*), 1)   AS pct_max
FROM contratos c LEFT JOIN m ON m.id_contrato = c.id_contrato
GROUP BY c.modalidad_familia;

Chart 4: bodies with the most additions (sql/04_adiciones_por_entidad.sql)

WITH n_adic AS (
  SELECT id_contrato, COUNT(*) AS n FROM adiciones
  WHERE clase = 'adicion_valor' GROUP BY id_contrato
)
SELECT c.nombre_entidad AS entidad, COUNT(*) AS contratos,
  ROUND(100.0 * SUM(a.n IS NOT NULL) / COUNT(*), 1) AS pct_con_adicion
FROM contratos c LEFT JOIN n_adic a ON a.id_contrato = c.id_contrato
GROUP BY c.nit_entidad
HAVING COUNT(*) >= 300
ORDER BY pct_con_adicion DESC LIMIT 15;

Chart 1 note: the 96% of direct contracts (sql/05_justificacion_directa.sql)

SELECT justificacion_modalidad_de AS justificacion, COUNT(*) AS contratos,
  ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(*) / (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM contratos
        WHERE modalidad_familia = 'Directa'), 1) AS pct
FROM contratos WHERE modalidad_familia = 'Directa'
GROUP BY justificacion_modalidad_de ORDER BY contratos DESC LIMIT 8;

Share of value awarded directly, by body (sql/02_directa_participacion_valor.sql)

SELECT nombre_entidad AS entidad, COUNT(*) AS contratos,
  SUM(CASE WHEN modalidad_familia = 'Directa' AND NOT flag_valor_no_positivo
           THEN valor_del_contrato END) AS valor_directa
FROM contratos GROUP BY nit_entidad HAVING COUNT(*) >= 300;

So what

Colombia Compra Eficiente, the national procurement agency, should make the amendment type and the added amount required fields.

Recommendation

Today, anyone checking contract growth from open data gets a figure about six times too low. One required field would make the right number one query away.

What this does not say

Direct awards and additions are legal tools, not proof of wrongdoing.

Show the evidence
  • SECOP I is not included: only SECOP II.
  • Contracts signed in 2025 have had less time to be amended, so their rate will still rise.
  • The classifier is rule-based. The gap between 13.7% and 20.0% is what it cannot decide.
  • The amendments dataset has no amount field, so the growth in pesos cannot be summed.
